Ten Asian American young adults come together in a Koreatown house in Los Angeles, navigating dating, friendships, attraction, conflict, nightlife, culture and the unpredictable relationships that form when their lives become intertwined.

Baesians is an unscripted reality dating and lifestyle series following ten Asian American young adults as they live, socialize and explore Los Angeles together. Based in Koreatown, the series moves between the house and real-world dates, events, activities, nightlife and cultural experiences as friendships deepen, romantic connections emerge and relationships evolve throughout the season.

Why Baesians
01

Asian American at the Center

An Asian American ensemble is the primary cast and social world of the series, not a secondary element.

02

Koreatown and greater Los Angeles are an active backdrop for dating, nightlife, food, events and cultural experiences.

04
